# Primary Paper Catalog

Curated papers that constrain NSRL's claims or directly inform its next
experiments. Links point to primary records. `Float boundary` describes what
remains outside the integer or quantized path; it is deliberately conservative.

Last reviewed: **2026-07-15**.

## Reading priority

Read these first for the current roadmap:

1. **Direct Quantized Training**, **ECO**, and **Ternary Momentum** for the
   newest direct-parameter and quantized-optimizer comparisons.
2. **WAGE**, **NITI**, and **Is Integer Arithmetic Enough?** for the strongest
   prior art against broad integer-training novelty claims.
3. **Deep Learning with Limited Numerical Precision**, **Error Feedback Fixes
   SignSGD**, and **Overcoming Oscillations in QAT** for update dead zones,
   rounding, residual carry, and lattice oscillation.
4. **Quantization-Aware Scaling**, **AMPA**, **SwitchBack**, and **Jetfire** for
   per-tensor/per-layer control at larger scale.
5. **Training Quantized Nets: A Deeper Understanding**, **Understanding STE**,
   **QuEST**, **LOTION**, **StochasticAD**, and the two discrete
   **Faà di Bruno** papers for the theory behind unreachable, surrogate, and
   exactly compositional finite-difference directions.
6. **Pseudo-Boolean Optimization**, **Discrete Convex Analysis**, the
   bounded-treewidth elimination paper, **granular MADS**, and **Adaptive
   Sparse Möbius Transforms** for deciding whether a move cube admits a
   certified optimizer or only heuristic search.
7. The two **Ramanujan Sums in Signal Processing** papers, the
   **Green--Tao arithmetic regularity lemma**, and the finite-group **U3 inverse
   theorem** for exact phase/action diagnostics and their limitations.
8. **Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis** for the statistical firewall
   around repeated structure discovery and candidate selection.
9. **Fast Weight Programmers** and the 2026 **TTT/linear-attention** paper for
   the boundary between parameter updates, learned memory, and recurrent state.

## Native and integer-oriented training

| [Training and Inference with Integers in Deep Neural Networks (WAGE)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HJGXzmspb) (Wu et al., 2018) | ICLR 2018 | Quantizes weights, activations, gradients, and errors into low-bit integers; replaces BatchNorm with constant scaling | Experiments simulate the discrete algorithm on conventional training hardware | Major missing predecessor: discrete forward and backward dataflow predates NSRL. | [NITI: Training Integer Neural Networks Using Integer-only Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13108) (Wang et al., 2020) | arXiv preprint; open-source implementation | Integer parameters/intermediates and integer arithmetic; pseudo-stochastic rounding; 8-bit datapath with wider accumulation | Offline evaluation and hardware environment are not `no_std`; ImageNet uses 16-bit weight accumulation | Closest systems prior for integer backpropagation, rounding, and accumulator-width choices. |
| [Octo: INT8 Training with Loss-aware Compensation and Backward Quantization](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc21/presentation/zhou-qihua) (Zhou et al., 2021) | USENIX ATC 2021 | INT8 forward and backward for on-device learning with compensation and range clipping | Paper is a quantized-training system; inspect implementation before claiming every auxiliary operation is integer | Compensation layers and backward range control are alternatives to globally widening the model. |
| [PocketNN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.02863) (Song & Lin, 2022) | tinyML Research Symposium 2022 / arXiv | Integer-only training and inference in C++ using direct feedback alignment and integer activations | No float training path claimed; limited to small MLP classification | Establishes native integer training on embedded-class targets, but not backpropagation or sequence modeling. |
| [Is Integer Arithmetic Enough for Deep Learning Training?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08822) (Ghaffari et al., 2022) | NeurIPS 2022 | Integer forward, backpropagation, BatchNorm/LayerNorm, momentum, weight decay, and SGD using dynamic fixed-point mapping and stochastic rounding | Per-tensor scale selection is derived from exponent/range information; implementation and accelerator environment are not NSRL's static runtime contract | Strongest broad prior: includes large vision tasks and vision transformers. NSRL's novelty must be narrower than “integer backprop on a transformer.” |
| [On-Device Training Under 256KB Memory](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/90c56c77c6df45fc8e556a096b7a2b2e-Abstract-Conference.html) (Lin et al., 2022) | NeurIPS 2022 | 8-bit quantized transfer learning with Quantization-Aware Scaling and sparse updates | Starts from a pretrained model and selectively updates it; not native from-scratch integer training | Direct prior for gradient-scale calibration and freezing most of a trunk on constrained devices. |
| [Training Integer-Only Deep Recurrent Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11791) (Partovi Nia et al., 2022) | arXiv preprint | QAT produces integer-only recurrent inference with LayerNorm, attention, and piecewise-linear activations | Training is explicitly quantization-aware rather than a no-float native path | Relevant sequence-model evidence, but not a native integer-training precedent. |
| [TIFeD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16442) (Colombo et al., 2024) | AI-ML Systems 2023 / arXiv revision 2024 | Integer-only federated learning with direct feedback alignment, including single-layer distributed training | Small classification setting and DFA rather than end-to-end backpropagation | Supports layer-local training and distributed integer aggregation as embedded alternatives. |
| [NITRO-D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11698) (Pirillo et al., 2024) | arXiv preprint / later journal version | Native integer CNN training with local error signals, scaling layers, and IntegerSGD | Uses local losses rather than global backpropagation; vision only | Demonstrates depth through local integer learning and explicit amplification rather than a single global backward scale. |

| [A discrete Faà di Bruno's formula](https://doi.org/10.55016/ojs/cdm.v7i2.62119) (Duarte & Torres, 2012) | Contributions to Discrete Mathematics 2012 | Exact finite-difference composition formula for vector arguments and values | Algebraic theory rather than a quantized optimizer | Stable primary precedent for a chain rule based on finite differences rather than derivatives. |

| [Training Deep Neural Networks with 8-bit Floating Point Numbers](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/hash/335d3d1cd7ef05ec77714a215134914c-Abstract.html) (Wang et al., 2018) | NeurIPS 2018 | FP8 training with chunk-based accumulation and stochastic rounding | Floating-point format, not integer | Control showing that accumulation geometry matters independently of integer versus float representation. |
| [High-Accuracy Low-Precision Training (HALP)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03383) (De Sa et al., 2018) | arXiv / systems literature | Low-precision SGD with variance reduction and bit centering | Algorithm uses higher-level variance-reduction structure; not a native integer stack | Bit centering is prior art for moving the representable lattice around the current iterate. |
| [A geometric integration approach to smooth optimisation: Foundations of the discrete gradient method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.06444) (Ehrhardt et al., 2018) | arXiv preprint / optimization theory | Discrete gradients obey an exact objective-difference identity; includes derivative-free Itoh--Abe schemes | Convergence theory assumes a continuous objective rather than a deterministic integer program | Useful descent-identity precedent, but not the boundary-aware chain rule NSRL needs. |
| [Error Feedback Fixes SignSGD](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/karimireddy19a.html) (Karimireddy et al., 2019) | ICML 2019 | Stores compression error and reinjects it into the next update | Theory is about compressed gradients, usually represented around a float optimizer | NSRL residual carry is an application of established error-feedback principles, not an unpublished category. |
| [ARM: Augment-REINFORCE-Merge Gradient for Stochastic Binary Networks](https://openreview.net/forum?id=S1lg0jAcYm) (Yin & Zhou, 2019) | ICLR 2019 | Unbiased antithetic gradient estimator for expectations over stochastic binary variables | Optimizes distribution parameters in a conventional differentiable stack | Primary precedent for two-evaluation common-random-number probes of a Boolean move distribution. |
| [SWALP](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/yang19d.html) (Yang et al., 2019) | ICML 2019 | Quantizes weights, gradients, accumulators, and updates to 8 bits, then averages iterates | Evaluation stack is conventional ML software; averaging state may use a distinct format | Temporal averaging is another way to recover accuracy unavailable to one low-precision iterate. |
| [Understanding Straight-Through Estimator in Training Activation Quantized Neural Nets](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Skh4jRcKQ) (Yin et al., 2019) | ICLR 2019 | Analyzes surrogate gradients through piecewise-constant quantizers | Theoretical model, not integer-only execution | A surrogate backward can be a descent direction, but a poor STE can destabilize near minima. NSRL's STE paths need objective-alignment tests. |
| [Dimension-Free Bounds for Low-Precision Training](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/d4cd91e80f36f8f3103617ded9128560-Abstract.html) (Li & De Sa, 2019) | NeurIPS 2019 | Convergence analysis for quantized and logarithmic low-precision SGD | Theory abstracts away a concrete integer runtime | Useful starting point for formalizing scale, variance, and model-size effects without assuming a dimension penalty. |
| [A Statistical Framework for Low-bitwidth Training](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/099fe6b0b444c23836c4a5d07346082b-Abstract.html) (Chen et al., 2020) | NeurIPS 2020 | Treats fully quantized gradients as stochastic estimators and designs lower-variance quantizers | QAT reference remains high precision | Supplies the bias/variance language needed to compare deterministic rounding with stochastic rounding and error feedback. |
| [Toward Efficient Low-Precision Training: Data Format Optimization and Hysteresis Quantization](https://openreview.net/forum?id=3HJOA-1hb0e) (Lee et al., 2022) | ICLR 2022 | Layer-format selection plus hysteresis for quantized weights | Typical method retains a master copy; not native integer-only training | Direct prior for preventing one-grid-cell oscillation and for measuring format suitability before full training. |
| [Overcoming Oscillations in Quantization-Aware Training](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/nagel22a.html) (Nagel et al., 2022) | ICML 2022 | Diagnoses weights oscillating between adjacent grid points and proposes dampening/freezing | QAT with floating-point latent weights | Closest empirical precedent for quantization equivalence classes and late-stage integer update churn. |
| [Automatic Differentiation of Programs with Discrete Randomness](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/43d8e5fc816c692f342493331d5e98fc-Abstract-Conference.html) (Arya et al., 2022) | NeurIPS 2022 | Compositional stochastic derivatives yield unbiased estimators of derivatives of expected discrete programs | Does not automatically cover deterministic continuous-to-discrete thresholds; smoothed reverse mode has additional unbiasedness conditions | Shows that alternative discrete paths can be propagated and pruned compositionally instead of treating every discrete event as an STE. |
| [Scaling Laws for Precision](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/b2cac94f82928a85055987d9fd44753f-Abstract-Conference.html) (Kumar et al., 2025) | ICLR 2025 | Fits precision-aware scaling laws across more than 465 pretraining runs, up to 1.7B parameters and 26B tokens | Empirical scaling study across low-precision regimes; not a native integer optimizer | Low precision can reduce effective parameter count. NSRL scaling claims need to condition on precision, not raw parameter count alone. |
| [Stochastic Rounding for LLM Training: Theory and Practice](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v258/ozkara25b.html) (Ozkara et al., 2025) | AISTATS 2025 | Analyzes stochastic rounding under Adam and evaluates distributed BF16+SR pretraining through 6.7B parameters | Mixed-precision floating-point training, not a deterministic integer runtime | Seeded stochastic rounding is a theoretically motivated control, not merely a noise ablation; compare expected-update fidelity, replay contract, and validation loss. |
| [QuEST: Stable Training of LLMs with 1-Bit Weights and Activations](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/panferov25a.html) (Panferov et al., 2025) | ICML 2025 | QAT with Hadamard normalization, MSE-optimal fitting, and a trust gradient estimator; stable experiments down to 1-bit | The estimator explicitly approximates an unknown full-precision gradient and uses a conventional training stack | Direct support for making coarse-gradient alignment a measured optimization object rather than assuming that an STE is trustworthy. |
| [Direct Quantized Training of Language Models with Stochastic Rounding](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v304/zhao26b.html) (Zhao et al., 2025) | ACML 2025 | Directly updates low-precision language-model weights without unquantized STE weights; uses stochastic rounding and includes ternary and 8-bit lanes | Gradients, optimizer/support arithmetic, and BF16/FP8 environments are not claimed to satisfy a native integer-only contract | Strong direct-language-model predecessor. Seeded stochastic rounding is now a mandatory control for NSRL's deterministic residual updates. |
| [LOTION: Smoothing the Optimization Landscape for Quantized Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08757) (Kwun et al., 2025) | NeurIPS 2025 OPT workshop / arXiv | Optimizes the expectation of quantized loss under unbiased randomized-rounding noise and proves global-minimum preservation under the paper's support conditions | Standard optimizers act on a continuous smoothed objective; experiments are not a native integer runtime | A principled alternative to searching the raw piecewise-constant loss. Its convex-combination proof motivates a vertex-exact smoothing of NSRL move cubes. |
| [ECO: Quantized Training without Full-Precision Master Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22101) (Nikdan et al., 2026) | arXiv preprint | Applies updates directly to quantized parameters and injects post-step quantization error into optimizer momentum; evaluated from 30M Transformers through billion-scale and MoE settings | Activations, gradients, and optimizer compute are not claimed integer-only; quantization includes FP8 and INT4 regimes | Closest recent analogue to residual compensation without master weights. NSRL must compare where error is stored, how scales vary, and what state precision remains. |
| [Ternary Momentum for Quantized Training](https://openreview.net/forum?id=A3mVmPlahU) (Bar et al., 2026) | TMLR 2026 | Quantized parameters with ternary gradient information and stochastic ternary momentum; expected updates match standard momentum under the paper's construction | Does not claim end-to-end integer forward/backward arithmetic for a from-scratch causal decoder | Quantized optimizer state and stochastic expected-update matching are direct controls for deterministic RHU plus residual carry. |
| [Discrete Faà di Bruno via Möbius Inversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07742) (Hartmann, 2026) | arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-07-08 | Exact Boolean, multi-index, and iterated finite-difference chain rules for arbitrary maps between abelian groups | Covering counts grow combinatorially; no large-model optimization result | Supplies the exact composition law for a low-rank Boolean jet through quantizers, clipping, and integer maps. Treat as a fresh theorem source, not validated optimizer evidence. |

## Discrete black-box and pseudo-Boolean optimization

| Work | Status | Structural result | NSRL takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| [The basic algorithm for pseudo-Boolean programming revisited](https://doi.org/10.1016/0166-218X(90)90142-Y) (Crama, Hansen, & Jaumard, 1990) | Discrete Applied Mathematics 1990 | Recursive variable elimination is linear-time on the studied bounded-treewidth graph class | Interaction width, rather than polynomial degree alone, can make exact move selection tractable. Audit the primal support graph before using brute force. |
| [Pseudo-Boolean optimization](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-218X(01)00341-9) (Boros & Hammer, 2002) | Discrete Applied Mathematics 2002 survey | Organizes algebraic, exact, approximation, and special-class methods for polynomial set functions | NSRL move cubes are established pseudo-Boolean objects. Boolean-jet terminology must not obscure known submodular, quadratic, or factorized cases. |
| [Discrete Convex Analysis, Chapter 6: M-Convex Functions](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9780898718508.ch6) (Murota, 2003) | SIAM monograph | The M-convex exchange axiom yields a local optimality criterion for global optimality | “Prime as local minimum” gains global force only after an exchange property is established on the relevant fixed-cardinality domain. |
| [Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random Variables](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830) (Hoeffding, 1963) | Journal of the American Statistical Association 1963 | Gives exponential concentration bounds for sums of independent bounded variables | Supplies a conservative simultaneous coefficient certificate and exposes the exponential-in-order sample cost of worst-case high-order Möbius inference. |
| [Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/bad5f33780c42f2588878a9d07405083-Abstract.html) (Dwork et al., 2015) | NeurIPS 2015 | Gives validity mechanisms for adaptively selected analyses and formalizes why ordinary holdout reuse overfits | Boolean support discovery is adaptive analysis. Completed transfer data cannot be recycled into later proposal design under ordinary fixed-test guarantees. |
| [The Mesh Adaptive Direct Search Algorithm for Granular and Discrete Variables](https://doi.org/10.1137/18M1175872) (Audet, Le Digabel, & Tribes, 2019) | SIAM Journal on Optimization 2019 | Extends MADS polling and mesh refinement to granular and discrete variables | Supplies a disciplined black-box fallback when no tail, exchange, or low-width certificate holds; it does not by itself provide an NSRL global guarantee. |
| [Beyond hypergraph acyclicity: limits of tractability for pseudo-Boolean optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23045) (Del Pia & Khajavirad, 2024) | arXiv preprint | Relates pseudo-Boolean tractability and intractability to signed-hypergraph structure, treewidth, and distance from acyclicity | Sparse-looking coefficients are insufficient: the topology of overlapping interactions controls whether exact optimization scales. |
| [Time-uniform, nonparametric, nonasymptotic confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240) (Howard et al., 2021) | Annals of Statistics 2021 / arXiv | Develops nonasymptotic confidence sequences valid uniformly over time, including empirical-Bernstein constructions | Candidate monitoring can be anytime-valid under the paper's sampling conditions; confidence sequences do not authorize adaptive reuse of an already exposed transfer set. |
| [Adaptive Sparse Möbius Transforms for Learning Polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06246) (Erginbas et al., 2026) | arXiv preprint, submitted 2026-02-05 | Exactly learns an `s`-sparse degree-`d` Boolean polynomial with adaptive group-testing queries; the fully adaptive bound is `O(sd log(n/d))` queries | Closest algorithmic precedent for compressed Boolean-jet discovery, but exact sparsity and selected-vertex query access must be replaced by approximate-tail and clustered-sampling guarantees. |

| Work | Status | Structural result | NSRL takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ramanujan Sums in the Context of Signal Processing, Part I](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSP.2014.2331617) and [Part II](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSP.2014.2331624) (Vaidyanathan, 2014) | IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing 2014 | Constructs integer-basis exact-period subspaces and orthogonal finite-duration decompositions; projection matrices are integer up to an overall scale | Supplies exact dyadic-period features for Q32-to-Q20 phase. | [An arithmetic regularity lemma, associated counting lemma, and applications](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2028) (Green & Tao, 2010; corrected 2020) | Published article / corrected arXiv version | Decomposes bounded functions into structured, small-`L2`, and Gowers-uniform components for arithmetic counting results under stated conditions | Useful structure-versus-randomness model, but small Gowers norm does not bound a worst-vertex optimization excursion. | [The inverse theorem for the `U3` Gowers uniformity norm on arbitrary finite abelian groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13759) (Jamneshan & Tao, 2023) | Published version / arXiv | Gives quantitative and qualitative inverse results for large `U3` norm on arbitrary finite abelian groups | A large rank-six residual `U3` can motivate exact quadratic-phase inspection. ## Explicit gaps

This catalog did **not** find a peer-reviewed result that simultaneously
demonstrates all of the following:

- a causal language decoder trained from scratch;
- end-to-end integer forward, backward, and optimizer arithmetic;
- no floating-point master weights or optimizer state;
- deterministic replay across parallel and resumed training;
- and competitive held-out language-model quality at multi-million-parameter
  scale without retrieval or a float-trained teacher.

That conjunction is the credible open space for NSRL. Absence from this curated
catalog is not proof of absence from the literature; novelty language still
requires a dedicated systematic review before publication.
