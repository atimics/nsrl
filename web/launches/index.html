02 Six atoms enter the Forge

The useful result was “no.”

An atom is one small, reversible model change. We froze six of them, tried every combination, and kept the result that stopped the next expensive run.

  1. 01

    Freeze

    Lock six ordered moves, two exact objectives, the data boundary, and the checker.

  2. 02

    Try everything

    Evaluate all 64 subsets over 64 proposal documents: 8,192 exact forwards.

  3. 03

    Keep the “no”

    The compact rule chose a move already falsified on unused data. No scaling authorized.

What the cube actually says Structure observed

Interesting map. Rejected destination.

At Q32 precision, only 16 of 409,784 units of nonconstant interaction mass sat above order three. In plain language: this observed loss map was almost cubic.

But the exact support was still maximally tangled, every proposal document came from one source cluster, and the selected all-atom move had already lost its prospective test. That is a lead to replicate, not a performance breakthrough.

07 Gap analysis

The proof layer is real. The market is not—yet.

The signed localnet now covers identity, replay protection, role checks, validator quorum, challenges, and simulated settlement. It deliberately stops before wallets, custody, transferable assets, or consensus.
