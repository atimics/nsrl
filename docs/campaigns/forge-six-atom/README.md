# Forge six-atom campaign

Status: evidence-integrated launch package for The Crowley Bard account.

## Campaign objective

Make Forge legible to curious builders, reproducibility researchers, and
technical sponsors by showing one complete research loop:

```text
hypothesis -> frozen experiment -> falsifier -> checked outcome -> reusable artifact
```

The six-atom experiment is the flagship because it did not produce a new model
performance claim. The proposal found interesting aggregate structure, pointed
back to a move that had already failed prospectively, and stopped paid scaling.
A frozen confirmation then supported three narrow within-source actions while
falsifying the stronger pairwise and Gibbs parameter-map stories. The cleanest
surviving mechanism is a routed conditional exchange, not a global winner.

## Public promise

> Six atoms enter. All 64 combinations leave receipts.

Forge turns a mathematical hunch into a reproducible experiment, a falsifiable
bounty, and a hash-bound model artifact recorded through signed launch events.
Success, failure, and uncertainty all remain publishable outcomes.

## Audience

- Curious technical followers: “Show me why this experiment matters without
  making me decode the paper first.”
- Researchers and builders: “Give me the frozen contract, code, hashes, and
  exact next question.”
- Potential sponsors: “Let me fund a measurable objective without buying a
  promotional promise.”

## Message ladder

1. **Hook:** Six small model changes create 64 possible combinations. Forge
   tested all of them.
2. **Stakes:** The compact mathematical answer looked promising but selected a
   move already falsified on unused data.
3. **Plot twist:** A frozen follow-up found a narrow routed exchange, while the
   pairwise and Gibbs maps that generated two actions failed to replicate.
4. **Method:** Freeze the claim, data boundary, checker, success test, and
   falsifier before promotion.
5. **Artifact:** Publish the code, hashes, outcome, and signed coordination
   record so someone else can replay or challenge it.
6. **Invitation:** Independently replay the audit and confirmation, preserve
   the sealed range, design the missing multi-source test, and publish any
   checked outcome.

## Tone for The Crowley Bard

The voice is curious, theatrical, and slightly mischievous. The evidence stays
literal.

- Lead with a scene: atoms enter a forge; every combination leaves a receipt.
- Let one vivid line carry each post. Do not stack metaphors.
- Translate the noun before using it: an “atom” is one small, reversible model
  change.
- Make the failed headline the punchline, never the researchers or audience.
- Keep occult flavor as seasoning. A reader should not need Crowley lore to
  understand the science.
- Use at most two emoji per post and never make emoji carry meaning on their
  own.

## Campaign success measures

Prioritize signals that indicate comprehension and participation:

- outbound clicks to the Forge experiment section and frozen audit;
- qualified replies that restate the method, propose a falsifier, or ask about
  reproduction;
- challenge reports, independent replays, or issue links;
- saves/bookmarks and thread completion when the platform exposes them; and
- corrections prompted by public scrutiny.

Likes and raw impressions are secondary. No result is promoted because a post
performs well.

## Accessibility standard

- Every image gets the supplied human-written alt text.
- Every quantitative claim also appears as post text, not only in the image.
- Use sentence case and CamelCase hashtags such as `#OpenScience`.
- Avoid all-caps sentences, decorative Unicode alphabets, long acronym chains,
  and emoji-only replies.
- Use short paragraphs with one idea each.
- Expand Q32 in replies as “the higher-precision integer objective” unless the
  exact representation is the subject.
- Never describe the 16-of-409,784 tail observation as proof that the structure
  generalizes. The experiment has one source cluster.

## Publication boundaries

- Say **candidate rejected**, **structure observed**, and **open challenge**.
- Do not describe the result as a breakthrough, solved problem, general proof,
  decentralized network, escrowed cash bounty, live token, or production
  market.
- Forge v1 is a signed single-process localnet with simulated,
  non-transferable credits. It has no wallet, custody, consensus, or financial
  settlement.
- The original six-atom structure audit is proposal-only. Its frozen
  confirmation is within one source cluster. Neither authorizes optimizer
  changes or paid scaling.
- A clean checkout replays the hash-bound cubes and derived results. The model
  and corpus bytes used for the original exact forwards are not repository
  artifacts; say this plainly when describing reproducibility.
