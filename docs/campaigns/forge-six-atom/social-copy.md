## D+3: what “falsifiable bounty” means

> A Forge bounty is not “make the model better somehow.”

> • one metric
> • a baseline and target
> • non-regression guardrails

## Reply bank

### “So did the model get better?”

> No new deployed-model performance improvement is claimed. Frozen actions won
> narrow within-source comparisons, but their pairwise and Gibbs explanations
> changed on confirmation. The checked decision remains: do not scale.

### “Why celebrate a failed candidate?”

> Because it prevented an unsupported claim and a larger run. A reproducible
> negative result narrows the search space and gives the next experiment a real
> starting point.

### “Is the bounty live money?”

> No. Forge v1 uses simulated, non-transferable credits in a single-process
> signed localnet. It has no wallet, custody, consensus, or financial
> settlement.

### “What makes it reproducible?”

> The audit binds the model, tokenizer, token stream, corpus index, move family,
> Rust source, executable, objectives, checker, result hashes, and data
> boundaries. The repository publishes the replay bindings.

### “What should I try next?”

> First independently replay the frozen audit and confirmation. Then freeze a
> multi-source test: does the conditional-exchange partition survive across
> source clusters? Publish negative and inconclusive outcomes too.
